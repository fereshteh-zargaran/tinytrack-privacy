Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

TinyTrack is a baby care tracking app that helps parents log feeding sessions, sleep, diaper changes, medicine, and daily routines. Your privacy is a priority — this policy explains exactly how the app handles your information.

Information You Enter

TinyTrack stores information you choose to enter, including:

  • Baby name
  • Feeding logs and session durations
  • Sleep records
  • Diaper change entries
  • Medicine records
  • Reminder preferences

How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored entirely on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and local storage (UserDefaults). TinyTrack does not transmit your data to any server, and no data leaves your device.

🔒 Data shared between the app and its home screen widget is kept within a local App Group container, also on your device only.

Notifications

TinyTrack uses Apple's local notification system to send you feeding and care reminders. These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device. No notification content is sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services

TinyTrack does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. The app uses only Apple system frameworks.

Data Sharing

TinyTrack does not collect, share, or sell any personal data to third parties. Your data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

TinyTrack is designed for use by parents and caregivers. The app does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
